The commentary's own renderings of the Greek are never placed inside another translation's quotation marks. They are labeled "literally," "or," or "my rendering," or are set in single quotation marks.

**Translations deliberately not used.** Several widely loved translations cannot be reproduced freely, because their publishers require permission for anything beyond limited quotation. Under this book's rule they are not quoted. They include the English Standard Version, the New American Standard Bible, the New Living Translation, the Amplified Bible, *The Message*, the New International Version, the NET Bible, the New King James Version, the Christian Standard Bible, and the New Revised Standard Version. The King James Version is in the public domain in most of the world, but in the United Kingdom it remains under a perpetual Crown patent, so this book uses Noah Webster's 1833 revision of it (WBS) instead. Readers who use these translations will find the same range of approaches among the free ones: formal translations (ASV, RV, YLT, LSV, ULB, and the WEB) alongside the ESV and NASB; readable modern translations (BSB, OEB, and FBV) alongside the NLT; and a translation that makes implied meaning explicit (T4T), as the Amplified Bible and *The Message* try to do.

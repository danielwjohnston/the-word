# Appendix C. Jude and 2 Peter: The Parallel Passages {.unnumbered}

This appendix sets the parallel passages side by side in the Literal Standard Version. The texts are generated directly from the LSV source files by `tools/make_parallels.py`, so they are exact. For discussion of the relationship between the two letters, see the Historical and Literary Introduction, §8.

**Summary of the evidence.** The two letters share a sequence of themes, images, and rare words. Three Greek words occur in the New Testament only in these two letters: συνευωχέομαι ("feast together"), ὑπέρογκος ("swollen"), and ἐμπαίκτης ("scoffer").[^ac-1] Jude contains material not found in 2 Peter: the quotation of Enoch (vv. 14–15), the named dispute of Michael and the Devil over Moses' body (v. 9), Cain and Korah (v. 11), the wild waves and wandering stars (v. 13), and the closing exhortations to mercy (vv. 20–23). 2 Peter adds the flood and Noah (2:5), Lot (2:7–8), Balaam's donkey (2:16), and the scoffers' question about Christ's coming (3:4).

## Jude 2 ∥ 2 Pet 1:2

> **2** mercy and peace and love be multiplied to you!
>
> — Jude 2, LSV

> **1:2** Grace and peace be multiplied to you in the acknowledgment of God and of Jesus our Lord!
>
> — 2 Pet 1:2, LSV

*Observation:* Same wish formula ("be multiplied"); Jude's triad differs.

## Jude 4 ∥ 2 Pet 2:1–3

> **4** for there came in certain men stealthily, having been written beforehand to this judgment long ago, impious, perverting the grace of our God to licentiousness, and denying our only Master and Lord—Jesus Christ.
>
> — Jude 4, LSV

> **2:1** But there also came false prophets among the people, as there will also be false teachers among you, who will stealthily bring in destructive sects, even denying the Master who bought them, bringing quick destruction to themselves, **2:2** and many will follow out their destructive ways, because of whom the way of the truth will be spoken of [as] evil, **2:3** and in covetousness, with forged words, they will make merchandise of you, whose judgment of old is not idle, and their destruction does not slumber.
>
> — 2 Pet 2:1–3, LSV

*Observation:* Intruders "came in … stealthily" / "will stealthily bring in"; both deny the Master (δεσπότης). Jude: past tense; 2 Peter: future.

## Jude 5 ∥ 2 Pet 1:12

> **5** Now I intend to remind you, you once having known all this, that Jesus, having saved a people out of the land of Egypt, He secondly destroyed those not having believed;
>
> — Jude 5, LSV

> **1:12** Therefore, I will always be ready to remind you about these things, although you have known [them] and have been established in the present truth,
>
> — 2 Pet 1:12, LSV

*Observation:* Both remind readers of what they already know.

## Jude 6 ∥ 2 Pet 2:4

> **6** messengers also, those who did not keep their own principality, but left their proper dwelling, He has kept in eternal bonds under darkness until [the] judgment of [the] great day,
>
> — Jude 6, LSV

> **2:4** For if God did not spare messengers having sinned, but having cast [them] down to Tartarus with chains of deepest gloom, delivered [them], having been reserved to judgment,
>
> — 2 Pet 2:4, LSV

*Observation:* Sinning angels kept in gloom for judgment. 2 Peter adds "Tartarus."

## Jude 7 ∥ 2 Pet 2:6–8

> **7** as Sodom and Gomorrah, and the cities around them, in like manner to these [messengers], having given themselves to whoredom, and having gone after other flesh, have been set before [as] an example, undergoing the justice of continuous fire.
>
> — Jude 7, LSV

> **2:6** and having turned the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah to ashes, condemned with an overthrow, having set [them as] an example to those about to be impious, **2:7** and He rescued righteous Lot, worn down by the conduct of the lawless in licentiousness, **2:8** for the righteous [man] dwelling among them was tormented in [his] righteous soul, day by day, in seeing and hearing unlawful works—
>
> — 2 Pet 2:6–8, LSV

*Observation:* Sodom and Gomorrah as example. 2 Peter adds the rescue of Lot and inserts the flood (2:5) to make a biblical sequence.

## Jude 8 ∥ 2 Pet 2:10

> **8** In like manner, nevertheless, those dreaming indeed defile the flesh, and they disregard lordship, and they speak evil of glorious [ones],
>
> — Jude 8, LSV

> **2:10** and chiefly those following after the flesh in lust [and] defilement, and despising lordship. Bold, self-pleased, they are not afraid to speak evil of glorious ones,
>
> — 2 Pet 2:10, LSV

*Observation:* Defiling the flesh, despising lordship, slandering "glorious ones."

## Jude 9 ∥ 2 Pet 2:11

> **9** yet Michael, the chief messenger, when disputing with the Devil, reasoning about the body of Moses, did not dare to bring against [him] a judgment of slander, but said, “The LORD rebuke you!”
>
> — Jude 9, LSV

> **2:11** whereas messengers, being greater in strength and power, do not bear a slanderous judgment against them before the LORD;
>
> — 2 Pet 2:11, LSV

*Observation:* Angels do not bring a slanderous judgment. 2 Peter omits Michael, the Devil, and Moses.

## Jude 10 ∥ 2 Pet 2:12

> **10** But these, indeed, whatever [things] they have not known, they speak evil of; and whatever [they know], like the irrational beasts, they understand naturally—in these they are corrupted;
>
> — Jude 10, LSV

> **2:12** and these, as irrational natural beasts, made to be caught and destroyed—in what things they are ignorant of, slandering—in their destruction will be destroyed;
>
> — 2 Pet 2:12, LSV

*Observation:* Slandering what they do not know; irrational beasts; destruction.

## Jude 11 ∥ 2 Pet 2:15–16

> **11** woe to them! Because they went on in the way of Cain, and they rushed to the deceit of Balaam for reward, and in the controversy of Korah they perished.
>
> — Jude 11, LSV

> **2:15** having forsaken a right way, they went astray, having followed in the way of Balaam the [son] of Bosor, who loved a reward of unrighteousness, **2:16** and had a rebuke of his own iniquity—a mute donkey, having spoken in man’s voice, forbid the madness of the prophet.
>
> — 2 Pet 2:15–16, LSV

*Observation:* Balaam and his reward. 2 Peter omits Cain and Korah and adds the donkey.

## Jude 12 ∥ 2 Pet 2:13; 2 Pet 2:17

> **12** These are stains at your love-feasts, feasting together with you without fear, shepherding themselves; waterless clouds, being carried away by winds; autumnal trees without fruit, having died twice, having been uprooted;
>
> — Jude 12, LSV

> **2:13** doing unjustly, [they will receive] a reward of unrighteousness, esteeming pleasure in the day, [and] luxury—[they are] spots and blemishes, reveling in their deceits, feasting with you, … **2:17** These are wells without water, and clouds driven by a storm, to whom the deepest gloom of darkness has been kept throughout the age;
>
> — 2 Pet 2:13; 2 Pet 2:17, LSV

*Observation:* "Feasting together" (only here in the NT); "stains/reefs" vs. "spots and blemishes"; "love-feasts" vs. "deceits" (a one-letter difference in Greek); waterless clouds vs. "wells without water, and clouds driven by a storm."

## Jude 13 ∥ 2 Pet 2:17

> **13** wild waves of a sea, foaming up their own shameful [deeds]; stars going astray, for whom the gloom of the darkness throughout the age has been kept.
>
> — Jude 13, LSV

> **2:17** These are wells without water, and clouds driven by a storm, to whom the deepest gloom of darkness has been kept throughout the age;
>
> — 2 Pet 2:17, LSV

*Observation:* "The gloom of the darkness … has been kept" applied in 2 Peter to the wells and mists.

## Jude 16 ∥ 2 Pet 2:18

> **16** These are discontented grumblers, walking according to their own lusts; and their mouth speaks swollen words, giving admiration to persons for the sake of profit;
>
> — Jude 16, LSV

> **2:18** for speaking swollen words of vanity, they entice in desires of the flesh—licentiousness, those who had truly escaped from those conducting themselves in error,
>
> — 2 Pet 2:18, LSV

*Observation:* "Swollen words" (ὑπέρογκα; only here in the NT).

## Jude 17–18 ∥ 2 Pet 3:1–3

> **17** but you, beloved, remember the sayings spoken before by the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ, **18** that they said to you, that in the last time there will be scoffers, following after their own lusts of impieties,
>
> — Jude 17–18, LSV

> **3:1** This [is] now, beloved, a second letter I write to you, in both which I stir up your pure mind in reminding [you] **3:2** to be mindful of the sayings said before by the holy prophets, and of the command of us the apostles of the LORD and Savior, **3:3** knowing this first, that there will come scoffers in the last days, going on according to their own desires,
>
> — 2 Pet 3:1–3, LSV

*Observation:* Remembering the apostles' words; scoffers in the last time/days following their own desires (ἐμπαῖκται; only here in the NT). 2 Peter adds the prophets and specifies mockery of the promise of Christ's coming.

## Jude 24–25 ∥ 2 Pet 3:17–18

> **24** And to Him who is able to guard you without stumbling, and to set [you] in the presence of His glory unblemished, in gladness, **25** to the only wise God our Savior, [is] glory and greatness, power and authority, both now and forever! Amen.
>
> — Jude 24–25, LSV

> **3:17** You, then, beloved, knowing before, take heed, lest, together with the error of the impious being led away, you may fall from your own steadfastness, **3:18** and increase in grace, and in the knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ; to Him [is] the glory both now, and to the day of the age! Amen.
>
> — 2 Pet 3:17–18, LSV

*Observation:* Both close with a warning against falling and a doxology. 2 Peter's doxology is addressed to Christ.

## Material in Jude without a parallel in 2 Peter

- The greeting's description of believers as called, loved, and kept (v. 1).
- The exodus example, with the reading "Jesus" (v. 5).
- The story of Michael and the Devil over Moses' body (v. 9).
- Cain and Korah (v. 11).
- "Wild waves of a sea" and "stars going astray" (v. 13).
- The quotation from 1 Enoch (vv. 14–15).
- "Grumblers" and flattery (v. 16).
- "Causing divisions … not having the Spirit" (v. 19).
- Building, praying in the Holy Spirit, keeping in God's love (vv. 20–21).
- Mercy for the doubting and rescue from the fire (vv. 22–23).

# Bibliography {.unnumbered}

The bibliography is divided according to how each work was used in preparing this book. **Section 1** lists the texts and editions that were retrieved and read directly; specific passages relied on were checked against them. **Section 2** lists works known only through reliable secondary reports. **Section 3** lists standard scholarly works cited for widely known positions that could not be re-consulted during the research for this edition. Claims attributed to Section 3 works should be checked against the printed volumes before publication, and page numbers have deliberately been omitted for them. Full details of the verification process are in the project file `research/source-ledger.md`.

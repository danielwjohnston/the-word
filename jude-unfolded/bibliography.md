# Bibliography {.unnumbered}

Every work listed here is in the public domain or under a free license, and every work listed was retrieved and read directly; the specific passages relied on were checked against it. Works that would have required permission to quote, and works that could not be consulted directly, have been removed from this edition (see "Sources not used" at the end). Full details of the verification process are in the project file `research/source-ledger.md`.

## 1. Manuscripts

## Sources not used

Earlier drafts of this book referred to modern commentaries, lexicons, grammars, and studies, among them the commentaries of Richard Bauckham, Peter Davids, Gene Green, J. N. D. Kelly, Jerome Neyrey, Thomas Schreiner, and others; Bruce Metzger's *Textual Commentary*; the BDAG lexicon; Daniel Wallace's grammar; and studies by Tommy Wasserman, Duane Watson, and J. T. Milik. These works are valuable, but they are under copyright and could not be consulted directly for this edition, so every claim that depended on them has been removed or re-established from the free sources above. Copyrighted Bible translations are likewise not quoted; see the Copyright and Licensing page.
